THE STORY BEHIND THE ’LOO

A neighborhood gathering place.

Our name was inspired by Waterloo Records, the independent Austin record store founded by owner Josh Karp’s father, Louis. That spirit lives on in downtown Louisville, with Texas-inspired food, music, and a rooftop patio right on Main Street.
